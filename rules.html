SAFE AI USAGE

RULES & SAFETY

Lumina is built for learning, creativity, productivity, and responsible AI use. Please follow these rules to keep the environment safe and respectful.

🚫

No Harmful Content

Do not use Lumina to create harmful, violent, illegal, hateful, abusive, or dangerous content. Lumina should be used to help, not harm.

🔒

Respect Privacy

Do not share private information about yourself or others unless it is necessary. Avoid entering passwords, OTPs, bank details, or personal documents.

⚠️

Do Not Upload Sensitive Data

Avoid uploading sensitive images, private files, confidential documents, identity proofs, or personal records into the app.

🎓

Educational & Creative Use

Use Lumina for studying, coding, brainstorming, writing, designing, image creation, voice generation, and productive work.

👤

Account Responsibility

You are responsible for your account activity. Keep your login secure, sign out on shared devices, and do not misuse the platform.

✅

Use AI Responsibly

AI can make mistakes. Always verify important information before using it for academic, professional, financial, medical, or legal decisions.

Our Goal

Lumina AI is designed to support safe learning, creativity, and productivity. By using Lumina responsibly, you help create a cleaner and safer AI environment.
